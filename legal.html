Legal

Policies & Terms

How we handle orders, deliveries, returns and your personal information.

Shipping Policy

We supply from Bulawayo. Items can be collected from our premises or delivered by arrangement. Delivery cost and timing depend on the location and the size of the order, and are confirmed with you before dispatch. Cut-to-size and edged boards are prepared to order, so lead times are confirmed when your order is accepted.

Returns Policy

Please check your goods on collection or delivery. If an item is faulty or not what you ordered, contact us within 7 days with your order details and we will repair, replace or refund it. Boards and other materials that were cut, edged or made to your measurements cannot be returned unless faulty. Items must be unused and in their original condition.

Terms of Sale

Prices and availability are shown as a guide and may change without notice. An order is confirmed only once we have accepted it and, where required, received payment or a deposit. Trade accounts are subject to approval and agreed terms. Colours and finishes may vary slightly from photographs and screen displays. We are not liable for loss arising from incorrect measurements supplied by the customer.

Privacy Policy

When you submit an enquiry or place an order we collect the details you give us, such as your name, phone number, email address and order information. We use them only to respond to you, process your order and arrange delivery. We do not sell your information. Enquiries and orders are stored securely with our service provider, Supabase. We use cookies to remember your preferences and keep your cart. You can ask us to see or delete your information at any time.

Questions
