Who We Are

Boards, Cutting
& Fittings Specialists

Armand Carpentry & Designs is Bulawayo's home for boards, cutting and fittings — from melamine, super wood and granite tops to the architectural hardware, kitchen fittings and construction fixings that finish the job.

Founded by Mandla Mkandla, Armand has grown from a specialist fittings shop into a one-stop source for boards, cutting, edging and fittings. We cut and edge boards to your sizes, and carry European-grade hinges, premium handle collections, complete drawer systems, security hardware, kitchen fitting solutions and construction fixings — all under one roof at Iversens Complex, Khami Road, Bulawayo.

Comprehensive Catalogue

Same-day collection from Bulawayo. Nationwide delivery to Harare, Gweru, Mutare and beyond via trusted couriers.
